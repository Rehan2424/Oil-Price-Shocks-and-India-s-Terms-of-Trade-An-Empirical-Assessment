# The Empirical Measurement of Intra-Industry Trade

Herbert G. Grubel and Peter J. Lloyd (1971), *Economic Record* 47(4), 494–517

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1475-4932.1971.tb00772.x)

## The idea

- Measure how much of a country's trade in an industry is two-way: GL = 1 − |X − M| / (X + M), from 0 (all one-way) to 1 (exports equal imports).
- The paper also discusses how the measure depends on how broadly industries are defined and on the country's overall trade imbalance.

## What we use from it

- The Grubel–Lloyd index from lectures 19 and 20, applied to India's oil trade with UN Comtrade data.
- Lumping crude and refined products together gives 0.55 (2025); measuring them separately gives 0.10. India's oil trade is really inter-industry: crude in, fuels out (the aggregation warning of lecture 17).
