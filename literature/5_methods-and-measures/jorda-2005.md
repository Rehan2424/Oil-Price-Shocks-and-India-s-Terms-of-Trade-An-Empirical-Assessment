# Estimation and Inference of Impulse Responses by Local Projections

Òscar Jordà (2005), *American Economic Review* 95(1), 161–182

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/0002828053828518)

## The problem it solves

- Impulse responses from a VAR depend on the whole model being right; a mistake at one lag spreads to every horizon.

## The method

- Estimate a separate regression for each horizon (the outcome h periods ahead on today's shock), instead of iterating one model forward.

## Why it helps

- Simple OLS, more robust to misspecification, easy inference, and easy to make nonlinear.

## What we use from it

- Our monthly timing results: India's terms of trade barely move in the month oil rises and fall about 0.39% per 1% Brent rise after two months (April 2019 to June 2026).
- Also our monthly check with Känzig's shocks.
