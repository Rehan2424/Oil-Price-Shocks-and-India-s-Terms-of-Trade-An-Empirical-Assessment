# Response Surface Regressions for Critical Value Bounds and Approximate p-values in Equilibrium Correction Models

Sebastian Kripfganz and Daniel C. Schneider (2020), *Oxford Bulletin of Economics and Statistics* 82(6), 1456–1481

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/obes.12377)

## The problem it solves

- The original bounds-test critical values are asymptotic, so they can mislead in small samples.

## The method

- Response surfaces fitted to about 95 billion simulated F-statistics and 57 billion t-statistics give critical values and p-values for any sample size, lag order and number of regressors.

## What they find

- With asymptotic critical values the bounds test can be oversized by more than 5 percentage points in small samples: it finds long-run relationships too often.

## What we use from it

- With only 54 years of data, we use finite-sample critical values and p-values: p = 0.025 against the I(0) bound and 0.070 against the I(1) bound.
