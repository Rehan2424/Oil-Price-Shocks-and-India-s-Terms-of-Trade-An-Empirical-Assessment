# Oil Shocks and External Balances

Lutz Kilian, Alessandro Rebucci and Nikola Spatafora (2009), *Journal of International Economics* 77(2), 181–194

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinteco.2009.01.001)

## The question

- How do oil demand and oil supply shocks affect the external balances of oil exporters and importers?

## How they answer it

- Structural oil-market shocks (supply, global demand, oil-specific demand) and their effect on the oil and non-oil trade balance, the current account and net foreign assets, for groups of countries.

## What they find

- The effect on the overall trade balance and the current account depends critically on how the non-oil trade balance responds.
- The answer differs by the kind of shock, and valuation effects cushion the impact on net foreign assets.

## What we use from it

- Two design choices: separate oil from non-oil trade (our benchmark uses oil's shares s_x and s_m) and separate supply from demand shocks (H3).
