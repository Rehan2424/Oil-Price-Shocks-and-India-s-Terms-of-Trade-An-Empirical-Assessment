# The Macroeconomic Effects of Oil Supply News: Evidence from OPEC Announcements

Diego R. Känzig (2021), *American Economic Review* 111(4), 1092–1125

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20190964) · [shock data (author's GitHub)](https://github.com/dkaenzig/oilsupplynews)

## The question

- How do changes in expectations about future oil supply affect oil prices and the economy?

## How he answers it

- High-frequency identification: moves in oil futures prices in a narrow window around OPEC announcements, used to build an "oil supply news" shock.

## What he finds

- Negative supply news raises oil prices immediately, lowers oil production gradually and raises inventories.
- US activity falls, prices and inflation expectations rise, and the dollar depreciates.

## What we use from it

- His shock series is our cross-check for H3: India cannot cause these shocks, so they answer the "India is not a price-taker" objection (L11).
- In our data one shock raises the oil price about 10% in the same month. India's terms of trade fall about 1% in annual data and 4–5% after two to three months in monthly data.
