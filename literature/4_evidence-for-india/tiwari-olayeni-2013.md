# Oil Prices and Trade Balance: A Wavelet Based Analysis for India

Aviral Kumar Tiwari and Olaolu Richard Olayeni (2013), *Economics Bulletin* 33(3), 2270–2286

[Paper](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ebl/ecbull/eb-13-00405.html)

## The question

- What is the lead–lag relationship between the real oil price and India's real trade balance, and does it differ across time horizons?

## How they answer it

- Monthly data, January 1980 to December 2011, with continuous wavelet methods (wavelet coherence and cohesion) that separate short and long cycles.

## What they find

- The correlation changes sign and strength across periods and horizons: positive at some scales and dates, strongly negative at others (for example 1990–97 at 1.75–2.25 years).
- Where the link is significant, the oil price leads the trade balance.

## What we use from it

- Evidence that short-run and long-run effects differ, which supports our error-correction model and the rolling-window estimates.
