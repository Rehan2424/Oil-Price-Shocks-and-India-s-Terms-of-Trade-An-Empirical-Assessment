# Oil Price Shock, Pass-through Policy and its Impact on India

N. R. Bhanumurthy, Surajit Das and Sukanya Bose (2012), NIPFP Working Paper No. 2012-99

[Paper](https://www.nipfp.org.in/media/documents/wp_2012_99.pdf)

## The question

- How do world oil prices, and India's policy of passing them on to domestic fuel prices, affect growth, inflation and the budget?

## How they answer it

- A structural macroeconomic policy-simulation model for India with three channels: imports, prices and the budget, simulated for 2012-13 to 2016-17.

## What they find

- A 10% rise in oil prices lowers growth by about 0.6% with partial pass-through and 0.9% with full pass-through.
- Deregulating fuel prices hurts growth and raises inflation in the short run, but can help in the medium run if subsidy savings go to capital spending.

## What we use from it

- How oil reaches the Indian economy (the import bill, prices, the budget), on our literature slide.
- Background for the policy slide: how fuel taxes and pass-through decide who absorbs an oil shock.
