# Examining Crude Oil Price – Exchange Rate Nexus for India during the Period of Extreme Oil Price Volatility

Sajal Ghosh (2011), *Applied Energy* 88(5), 1886–1889

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2010.10.043)

## The question

- How do oil price movements affect the rupee–dollar exchange rate?

## How he answers it

- Daily data from July 2007 to November 2008, with GARCH and EGARCH models of the exchange rate.

## What he finds

- A rise in the oil price return depreciates the rupee against the dollar.
- Positive and negative oil price shocks have similar effects on exchange-rate volatility, and the effect on volatility is permanent.

## What we use from it

- The exchange-rate channel, and why we do not control for the rupee: it reacts to oil itself, so controlling for it would absorb part of the oil effect.
- His symmetric finding is consistent with ours for H2.
