# The Impact of Global Oil Market Shocks on India's Merchandise Trade Balances

Abdhut Deheri and Sunit Kumar Sahu (2024), *Energy Research Letters* 5(3)

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.46557/001c.77528) · [free PDF](https://erl.scholasticahq.com/article/77528.pdf)

## The question

- How do different global oil market shocks affect India's aggregate, oil and non-oil trade balances?

## How they answer it

- A structural VAR with oil supply, global demand and oil-specific demand shocks.

## What they find

- Adverse oil-specific demand shocks worsen the aggregate and non-oil trade balances, while the oil trade balance improves.
- Among oil market shocks, only oil-specific demand shocks have a significant effect.

## What we use from it

- The closest study to ours, and the clearest view of the gap: it models India's trade balance, not its terms of trade.
- Like our H3 result, it finds that supply shocks are not the special danger for India.
