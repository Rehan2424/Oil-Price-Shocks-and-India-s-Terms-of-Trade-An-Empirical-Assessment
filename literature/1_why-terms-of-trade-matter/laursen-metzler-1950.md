# Flexible Exchange Rates and the Theory of Employment

Svend Laursen and Lloyd A. Metzler (1950), *Review of Economics and Statistics* 32(4), 281–299

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.2307/1925577)

## The question

- Do flexible exchange rates insulate a country from booms and slumps abroad?

## How they answer it

- A two-country Keynesian model in which spending depends on real income, and real income depends on the terms of trade.

## What they find

- No full insulation. When the terms of trade worsen, real income falls; households cut saving to smooth consumption, so imports do not fall enough and the trade balance deteriorates.
- This is the second half of the Harberger–Laursen–Metzler effect.

## What we use from it

- The link from our result to India's external accounts: an oil-driven fall in the terms of trade also weakens the current account.
- Cited on the literature slide, "why the terms of trade matter".
