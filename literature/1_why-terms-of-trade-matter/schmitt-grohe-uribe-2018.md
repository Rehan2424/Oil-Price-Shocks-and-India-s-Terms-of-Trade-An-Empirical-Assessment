# How Important Are Terms-of-Trade Shocks?

Stephanie Schmitt-Grohé and Martín Uribe (2018), *International Economic Review* 59(1), 85–111

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/iere.12263) · [free version (NBER)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w21253)

## The question

- Are terms-of-trade shocks really a major source of business cycles in emerging and poor countries, as calibrated models say?

## How they answer it

- Country-by-country structural VARs for 38 countries, then a three-sector business-cycle model estimated country by country.

## What they find

- Terms-of-trade shocks explain less than 10% of movements in aggregate activity in the SVARs.
- The models assign them a much larger role: a "disconnect" between theory and data.

## What we use from it

- The other side of the debate on our literature slide.
- Our viva answer (question bank C9): we study purchasing power, which is the terms of trade itself. Swings of 14–24% in one oil episode are large real-income shocks for India, whatever they do to output.
